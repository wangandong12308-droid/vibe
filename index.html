CHAMELEON

THE ART OF ADAPTATION

Chameleons are primarily arboreal, inhabiting trees and shrubs. Their grasping feet and, in many species, prehensile tails help them hold securely to branches.

Their eyes can move independently to survey their surroundings. During prey targeting, both eyes may coordinate toward the same subject. Vision plays a central role in hunting.

Changes in skin colour are associated with communication, physiological state, temperature and light. They do not simply reproduce every background.
