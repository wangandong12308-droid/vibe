## V6 antique materials

`assets/material-atlas-v6.png` was produced by the built-in imagegen tool from the two user-supplied antique-page photographs. The left panel is blank rag paper and the right panel aged calf leather. Code samples them as real colour and bump maps. All wording and ornamental frames are drawn separately in code.

Exact prompt:

Generate a material texture atlas with exactly TWO equal-width vertical panels touching at the centre, total landscape 3:2 ratio. No labels or text anywhere. Both panels are perfectly flat frontal material scans, not objects in perspective. LEFT PANEL: blank unprinted 17th-century handmade rag book paper, faithfully matching the reference photos' paper material only. Pale gray ivory near-monochrome, very tactile fine uneven cotton fibres and laid paper texture, natural broad diagonal wrinkles, softly compressed creases, small ancient pinholes and foxing specks, subtle gray brown oxidation concentrated on all four edges, worn thin corners, paper extends to all panel boundaries. Preserve the natural raised and depressed paper skin, but NO text, letters, symbols, print ghosts, coats of arms, ornamental frame, illustration or watermark. Neutral diffuse scanner illumination with believable micro shadow in the wrinkles, sufficient midtone variation to create a usable bump map. RIGHT PANEL: very old European calf leather book cover surface, dark chocolate walnut brown, irregular fine cracked grain, rounded broad recessed central rectangular panel with subtle blind embossed double rules, worn rubbed corners and exposed tan leather fibres along edges, patchy aged oxidation, small scuffs and faint scratches, restrained museum collection quality. NO writing, gold, heraldry, icons, straps, studs or background. Entire panel is leather. Photoreal, not procedural noise, not illustration, not shiny or plastic. This atlas supplies actual WebGL colour and bump textures; all typography will be rendered separately in code.
